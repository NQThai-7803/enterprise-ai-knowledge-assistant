## Remediation exit criteria — proposed gate, not a passed result

Any future correction must demonstrate, in separately authorized isolated tests and a **new** candidate: (1) no unauthorized evidence disclosure; (2) NO_ANSWER with zero citations when only unrelated visible evidence exists; (3) ANSWERED with material citation for relevant authorized evidence; (4) citation identity **and** question-material support; (5) no grounded-unrelated substitution; (6) preservation of mandatory conditions/exceptions; (7) prohibition/negation direction; (8) abstention on unresolved source authority without invented precedence; (9) valid Vietnamese and English paraphrases; (10) valid mixed-language answers in approved scope; (11) multi-document answers with each material source; (12) no benchmark/fixture hardcoding. Include revoked grant, non-READY, no-evidence, provider failure/timeout, SSE/non-stream parity, repaired-final-answer, and same-topic wrong-action/value controls where relevant. A correct NO_ANSWER on the negative alone is insufficient if positive controls collapse.

These are qualitative safety and acceptance behaviors grounded in the existing contracts and the observed failure. Proposed quantitative false-acceptance, false-abstention, latency, timeout and sample-size thresholds are **not** approved release policy; the product/security/release owners must set them before a future source correction is promoted. This report claims **none** of the exit criteria newly passed. The specific next action and stop rules are in [recommended engineering action](TASK-035.3D-next-engineering-action-20261014.md).
